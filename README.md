## Introduction
Personalized emails have a much higher conversion rate, but writing tailored icebreakers for every lead is time-consuming. This actor automates the process by taking a CSV or Google Sheets file of your leads and generating a unique, personalized icebreaker for each one, based on your custom prompt. Whether you're sending cold emails or scaling your outreach, this tool ensures you can engage leads with creativity and relevance.

### Why It’s Useful
- **Saves time**: Automates the creation of personalized messages for thousands of leads.  
- **Flexible personalization**: Use OpenAI models to guide the tone, style, and structure of your icebreakers.  
- **Easy integration**: Works with your existing lead lists (e.g., Apollo exports, CSVs, Google Sheets).  
- **Scalable**: Generate thousands of custom intros without additional manual effort.

## How It Works
1. **Upload Your Lead List**: Upload your CSV or Google Sheets file containing the leads you want to enrich.
2. **Map Your Columns**: Match your columns (e.g., company name, lead description) to guide the AI in personalizing each icebreaker.
3. **Input Your Personalization Prompt**: Define the tone and style of the icebreaker by writing a custom prompt or selecting a predefined one.
4. **Select OpenAI Model**: Choose the OpenAI model (e.g., GPT-4, GPT-3.5) based on the desired quality and your budget.
5. **Run the Actor**: Let the actor generate icebreakers for your entire lead list. When it's done, download the enriched CSV.

## FAQs

**What format should my lead list be in?**  
The actor accepts CSV files or publicly shared Google Sheets URLs. Ensure your file contains valid lead information, such as names, companies, and other key data.

**How does the AI create personalized icebreakers?**  
The AI uses your provided prompt to determine the tone and style of the icebreakers. You can guide it to create a friendly, professional, witty, or value-driven message.

**How do I choose the OpenAI model?**  
You can select from different models (GPT-4, GPT-3.5) depending on your quality needs and budget. Higher-quality models like GPT-4 generate more nuanced and contextually aware icebreakers.

**How long does it take to generate icebreakers?**  
While the actor processes leads in batches, results are typically available within 15–20 minutes, though larger batches might take up to 24 hours.
